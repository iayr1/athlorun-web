FAQ

Questions? We've got answers.

Is Athlorun free?

Yes. Athlorun is free to download and use. If we ever introduce optional paid features, they will be clearly labelled and priced in Google Play before you buy.

Which devices are supported?

Athlorun runs on Android phones. Step counting uses your phone's built-in sensors and, where available, Android Health Connect.

Why does the app ask for location, including in the background?

Location is used only to record the route, distance and pace of workouts you start. Background location lets tracking continue while your screen is off or you switch apps during an active workout. It is never used for ads and you can deny it — step counting still works.

What does Athlorun do with Health Connect data?

With your permission, Athlorun reads and writes steps and distance so your activity stays in sync with other health apps. This data is used only to show your progress and power in-app features. See our Health Data Policy.

How do I delete my account?

Open the app → Profile → Settings → Delete Your Account. You can also request deletion from our account deletion page without installing the app.

How can I contact support?

Email us at support@athlorun.com or use the contact form. We usually reply within 2 business days.
