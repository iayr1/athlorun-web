Delete your Athlorun account & data

You can permanently delete your Athlorun account and the data associated with it at any time — from inside the app, or using the form on this page without reinstalling the app.

App: Athlorun Developer: Athlorun Processed within 30 days

Option 1 — Delete in the app

  1. 1

    Open Athlorun

    Sign in to the account you want to delete.

  2. 2

    Go to Settings

    Tap your Profile, then the Settings icon.

  3. 3

    Delete Your Account

    Tap Delete Your Account and confirm by tapping Delete.

  4. 4

    Done

    You are signed out and your account is scheduled for deletion.

What gets deleted

  • Account and login (email, Google sign-in link)
  • Profile: name, photo, country, weight, goals
  • Health assessment answers
  • Steps, daily metrics, workouts and GPS routes
  • Gear, schedules, friends, badges, coins and leaderboard entries
  • Uploaded photos and notification preferences

What may be retained

We may keep a minimal record for up to 180 days where required by law or to prevent fraud — for example, records of reward redemptions for accounting, or a record that a deletion request was completed. Encrypted backups are overwritten within 90 days. Data you have written to Android Health Connect stays on your device under your control; remove it from Settings → Health Connect.

Coins and rewards are forfeited when an account is deleted, and deletion cannot be undone.

Option 2 — Request deletion from the web

Don't have the app installed? Submit this form. Your email app will open with a pre-filled request to support@athlorun.com — just press send. Please send it from the email address linked to your Athlorun account so we can verify you.

Partial deletion keeps your account active.

We'll confirm by email and complete the request within 30 days (usually much sooner).

Almost done! Your email app should have opened with your request. If it didn't, email support@athlorun.com with the subject "Account deletion request" and your account email address.
